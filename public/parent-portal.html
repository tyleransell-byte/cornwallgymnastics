Choose your sessions

Monthly booking: Choosing a Tuesday class books every Tuesday in your selected month. The same applies to Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday classes. All scheduled dates are included in one booking, not just one week.

For example, a Tuesday class in November 2026 includes 3, 10, 17 and 24 November. Each class covers every scheduled occurrence of its weekday in the chosen month (excluding confirmed club closures). Checkout is currently in Stripe test mode.
