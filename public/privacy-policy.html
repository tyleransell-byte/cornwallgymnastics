Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Who we are

Cornwall Gymnastics Centre (CGC), a Community Interest Company (company number 11984901), is responsible for personal information used in running our gymnastics activities.

Units 2 and 3, Knights Business Centre, Palmers Way, Trenant Industrial Estate, Wadebridge, Cornwall PL27 6HB.

For privacy enquiries, email hello@cornwallgymnastics.uk.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we collect parents’ and guardians’ names and contact details; children’s names, dates of birth and registration details; emergency contacts; relevant medical conditions, allergies and additional support needs; trial requests; bookings and payment records; and messages sent through the Parent Portal. We also process account and authentication information needed to operate the portal.

3. Why we use information

We use information to register children, arrange classes and trials, manage bookings and payments, contact parents, maintain class registers, support children’s welfare and safety, respond to emergencies, and operate and secure our services.

4. Lawful bases

Our reasons for processing information depend on its purpose and may include providing a service, complying with legal obligations, protecting vital interests, or legitimate interests in operating the centre safely. Health and medical information is specially protected under UK data protection law and requires an additional legal condition. Where consent is the basis for a particular use, it can be withdrawn.

Policy detail under review: We are documenting the precise lawful basis and special-category condition for each processing activity. Contact us if you would like information about the basis for a particular use.

5. Children’s medical and emergency information

We collect relevant medical, allergy, emergency contact and support information to help staff support children and respond appropriately in an emergency. Access should be limited to authorised staff who need the information for their responsibilities. Please keep your child’s details up to date.

6. Parent Portal

Our Parent Portal lets parents and guardians access information about children linked to their account, including registration, bookings, payments and requests. We use authentication and database access restrictions to help prevent access by unrelated parent accounts. Authorised staff may access information required for their duties.

7. Photos and videos

We take photographs and videos of gymnastics activities. These may be used for coaching, recording achievements or club communications, depending on the purpose and the permissions or other lawful basis that applies. Where consent is required, we will explain the intended use and how it can be withdrawn. Please contact us if you have questions or want to discuss photography permissions.

Policy detail under review: We are confirming how permissions are recorded and the circumstances in which images may be published on our website or social media.

8. Service providers and sharing

We use external services to operate the Parent Portal and payments, including Supabase for portal infrastructure and Stripe for online payment processing. Information may also be shared with authorised coaches and administrators, emergency services, safeguarding organisations, or authorities where necessary or required by law. We do not sell children’s personal information.

Policy detail under review: We are checking the full list of service providers and any international data transfers and safeguards.

9. How long we keep information

We delete children’s personal information after they leave the club, except where records must be retained for legal, safeguarding, accounting or other necessary reasons. Different records may be retained for different periods, after which they are securely deleted or anonymised.

Policy detail under review: Exact retention periods, including for backups, photographs and inactive portal accounts, are being confirmed. Contact us for information about a particular record.

10. Security

We use measures intended to protect information, including authenticated accounts and restricted access to relevant records. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you or your child may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; request portability; and withdraw consent where applicable. Children have their own data protection rights. Contact hello@cornwallgymnastics.uk to make a request.

12. Cookies and browser storage

Our website and Parent Portal may use browser storage and similar technologies needed for essential functions, including sign-in sessions.

Policy detail under review: We are checking whether additional cookies, analytics or tracking technologies are used and whether further choices or consent are needed.

13. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we use personal information, please contact us first so we can investigate. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

14. Updates

We may update this notice when our services or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be available here, and we will highlight significant changes where appropriate.
