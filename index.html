Why train with us

Experienced coaches

Every class is led by experienced coaches who help children learn new skills safely and build confidence at their own pace.

Professional equipment

Children train on professional gymnastics equipment, so they learn skills the right way from the very start.

A class for every age

From Stay and play for the very youngest, to recreational classes and invite-only WAG squads, there's a place for children up to 16.

Why join gymnastics

Strength and flexibility

Gymnastics works the whole body, building strength, flexibility and fitness.

Balance and coordination

Children learn to move with control, which helps in every other sport they try.

Confidence

Learning a new skill, then landing it, gives children a real sense of achievement.

Friends and fun

Classes are a great way to make friends, learn to listen and take turns, and enjoy being active.

Questions

How do I pay?

Stay and play, Adult Child Structured and Home Education are paid per class when you book. The other classes are booked as a monthly membership. Payments are handled by a secure payment provider, so we never see or store your card number.

Do I need to register my child?

Yes. Parents fill in a short registration form once, with their child’s details, before booking a first class.

Can we try a class first?

Yes. Use the Book a trial button at the top of the page.

What should my child wear?

Comfortable clothes they can stretch in, with no zips or buttons. Gymnastics is done barefoot. Long hair should be tied back.
